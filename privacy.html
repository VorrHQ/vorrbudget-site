Privacy

This page covers the vorrbudget.com website. The VorrBudget app's privacy policy will be added here before the app launches.

No cookies, trackers, or analytics

This website doesn't use cookies. It has no trackers, no analytics, and no ads. It doesn't load fonts, scripts, or anything else from other companies.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical records of visits, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure. We don't receive or use those records.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, your email address is sent to and kept by Buttondown, the service we use to send email. We only use it to tell you when VorrBudget launches. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Unsubscribing and deleting your email

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. You can also email hello@vorrbudget.com and ask us to remove your email address, and we'll delete it.

Questions

Email hello@vorrbudget.com.
