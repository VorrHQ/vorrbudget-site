Why we built VorrBudget

We lived paycheck to paycheck for years. We always knew what was due: the mortgage, the car note, the electric bill. What we didn't know was how to get there when every check came in different.

Budget apps didn't help. They all worked by the month, and we got paid by the week.

So we made a spreadsheet that planned one paycheck at a time. Each Friday, we could see exactly which bills that check had to cover, and what was left. The bills got paid on time. Then something we didn't expect happened: we started saving, and it kept growing.

VorrBudget is that spreadsheet, turned into an app. It does the math, warns you before a short week, and keeps your household on the same page.

If you get paid weekly or every two weeks, it was made for you.

What it does

  • Plans every bill on the paycheck before it's due, so nothing sneaks up on you.
  • Shows the weeks ahead in red and green, so you can see a short week coming and fix it early.
  • Handles hourly pay that changes week to week.
  • Shares one budget with your partner.
  • Never asks for your bank login. Your numbers are encrypted, and we don't sell your data or show ads.
