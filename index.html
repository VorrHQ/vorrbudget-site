What VorrBudget does

Know what's safe to spend

  • "Left to spend" counts the bills ahead.
  • See if a bill is due before your paycheck lands.
  • A week-by-week strip shows which weeks are covered and which are short.

Logging that takes seconds

  • Just say it (or type it): "50 gas, 200 groceries, mortgage paid."
  • Scan a receipt. It's read on your phone.
  • One-tap favorites, "Mark all paid," and a home-screen widget.
  • Paychecks count on payday automatically.

Built for two

  • One shared plan for your household.
  • Your own private what-ifs.

Plan ahead without guessing

  • Spread big bills across paychecks.
  • Try what-ifs: a raise, a new bill, paying off a debt early.
  • See your savings, your debts, and how many days ahead you are.

How VorrBudget protects you

  • We never ask for your bank login.

  • Your financial details are encrypted.

  • No ads, and we never sell your data.

  • When you import a statement, private details are covered on your phone before anything is sent. Receipts and bank files are read on your phone.

  • Our privacy policy says exactly what we store, in plain words. The app's privacy policy is in the app. This website has its own website privacy page.

How it's built
  • Every change is independently reviewed by a second reviewer whose job is to break it.
  • Stress tests try to make it show a wrong number.
  • The money math is checked thousands of ways against separately written models.
  • Your data is walled off from every other household.
